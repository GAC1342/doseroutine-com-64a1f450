**Formatting notes for App Store / Google Play:**

- The stores render plain text. Font size cannot be changed — hierarchy comes from **ALL-CAPS headers**, short lines, whitespace, emoji anchors, and 1-line power statements between sections.
- Keep the first 3 lines punchy — that's all users see before "more".
- Emoji act as visual bullets (F-pattern scanning). Do not overuse.
- Yearly listed **before** monthly (anchor + savings frame).
- "7-day free trial" repeated 3× (recall + risk reversal).
