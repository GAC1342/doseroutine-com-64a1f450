## Android settings that fix late or missing alerts

1. **Settings → Apps → [app] → Battery → Unrestricted** (or "Don't optimize").
2. Allow **Alarms & reminders** permission — required for exact scheduling on Android 12+.
3. Turn off manufacturer "deep sleep" or "sleeping apps" lists (Samsung Device Care, Xiaomi Autostart, OnePlus battery optimization).
4. Allow the app in **Do Not Disturb → Apps** so alerts sound.
5. Check the notification channel volume — Android sets importance per channel, not per app.

Do these in order; the first two fix the majority of complaints.

## Android vs iPhone

Feature sets are broadly similar. Android wins on widget flexibility and custom alarm sounds; iPhone wins on consistent notification behavior out of the box. See [the iPhone roundup](/articles/best-medication-reminder-apps-iphone) if you use both.

## FAQs

**What is the best medication reminder app for Android?**
DoseRoutine for several medications, supplements or injections; Medisafe when a caregiver needs alerting; a simple pill reminder for a single daily tablet.

**Why are my Android medication reminders late?**
Battery optimization. Set the app to unrestricted battery use and grant the "Alarms & reminders" permission, then remove it from any manufacturer sleeping-apps list.

**Do Android medication reminders work in Do Not Disturb?**
Only if you add the app to the Do Not Disturb exceptions and its notification channel is set to a high importance level.

**Is there a free Android medication reminder app?**
Yes, several. DoseRoutine is free to start with no card, and both Medisafe and MyTherapy have free tiers — compare them in [best free medication reminder apps](/articles/best-free-medication-reminder-apps).
