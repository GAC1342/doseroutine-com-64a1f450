# Best Apps for Tracking Supplements and Stacks

<p class="dr-speakable-answer">
The best supplement tracker apps in 2026 are DoseRoutine for full stacks with cycling and interaction awareness, Cronometer for micronutrient totals, MyFitnessPal for supplements alongside food, and generic reminder apps for simple daily vitamins. A supplement tracker differs from a pill reminder because it needs timing rules, cycling and interaction context, not just an alarm.
</p>

Supplements break most medication apps. Stacks change monthly, some items cycle on and off, several compete for absorption, and timing relative to food matters. Here is what handles that.

## What a real supplement tracker needs

- **Stack-level scheduling** — group items that are taken together.
- **Cycling** — 8 weeks on, 4 weeks off, without deleting and re-adding.
- **Timing rules** — with food, empty stomach, away from calcium or iron.
- **Interaction flags** — zinc and copper, calcium and levothyroxine, and similar.
- **Honest logging** — skipped days recorded, not silently ignored.

## 2. Cronometer — best for micronutrient totals

If your goal is hitting nutrient targets rather than adherence, Cronometer totals supplements alongside food. Weak on scheduling and cycling.

## 3. MyFitnessPal — best if food logging comes first

Supplements can be added as entries, but there is no timing or interaction logic.

## 4. Generic reminder apps — fine for a daily multivitamin

If your stack is one multivitamin and vitamin D, any reminder app works, including the ones in [best free medication reminder apps](/articles/best-free-medication-reminder-apps).

## How to structure a stack that sticks

1. **Group by timing, not by bottle** — morning empty stomach, morning with food, pre-bed.
2. **Separate the competitors** — keep calcium, iron and zinc at least two hours apart.
3. **Cycle deliberately** — set the off weeks in the app so you do not drift.
4. **Log skips** — a stack you take 60% of the time is a different experiment than one you take daily.
5. **Review monthly** — drop anything you cannot state a reason for.
