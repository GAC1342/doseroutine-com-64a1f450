# Best Medication Reminder Apps for iPhone

<p class="dr-speakable-answer">
The best medication reminder apps for iPhone in 2026 are DoseRoutine for multi-medication and supplement routines, Apple Health Medications for simple built-in reminders, Medisafe for caregiver alerts, and MyTherapy for adherence reports. On iOS the deciding factor is whether the app can send time-sensitive alerts that break through Focus modes.
</p>

iPhone reminders fail for one reason more than any other: Focus modes silence them. Before comparing apps, know that any app you choose needs notification permission plus time-sensitive alerts enabled.

## 1. DoseRoutine — best for complex routines

- Prescriptions, supplements and injections on one schedule.
- Time-sensitive alerts that survive Focus modes.
- Dose logging with amount and notes, plus exportable history.
- Reconstitution and dose math in the [calculator](/calculator).

Ranked first overall in [best medication reminder apps](/articles/best-medication-reminder-apps).

## 2. Apple Health Medications — best built-in option

Free, already installed, and integrated with the Health app so medications sit next to your other health data. Supports critical-style alerts and basic interaction warnings. Weak on supplements, injections and detailed logging. If Health sync is your priority, see [how to set up medication reminders in a health app](/articles/set-up-medication-reminder-health-app).

## 3. Medisafe — best for family alerts

Notifies a chosen contact when a dose is not confirmed. Free tier carries ads.

## 4. MyTherapy — best for clinician reports

Adherence percentage plus measurement logging and a printable summary.

## iPhone settings that stop missed reminders

1. **Settings → Notifications → [app] → Allow Notifications**, and enable **Time Sensitive Notifications**.
2. **Settings → Focus → [each Focus] → Apps** → add the app as allowed.
3. Keep **Scheduled Summary** off for the app; it batches alerts to a later time.
4. Turn on **Notification Grouping: Off** if you want each dose alert visible separately.
5. Check **Screen Time → Downtime** is not blocking the app.

Working through these in order fixes most cases. If alerts still do not arrive, the [reminder setup guide](/articles/set-up-medication-reminder-health-app) covers per-app steps.

## iPhone vs Android

The apps are largely the same across platforms; the failure modes differ. iOS problems are usually Focus and Scheduled Summary; Android problems are usually battery optimization. The [Android roundup](/articles/best-medication-reminder-apps-android) covers that side.

## FAQs

**What is the best medication reminder app for iPhone?**
DoseRoutine if you track several medications, supplements or injections; Apple Health Medications if you only need simple built-in reminders for one or two pills.

**Does the iPhone have a built-in medication reminder?**
Yes. The Health app includes a Medications section with schedules, reminders and basic interaction warnings, at no cost.

**Why do my iPhone medication reminders not appear?**
Almost always Focus mode, Scheduled Summary, or notifications not marked time-sensitive for that app. Enable time-sensitive alerts and allow the app in each Focus.

**Do these apps sync with Apple Health?**
Several do, including DoseRoutine, so doses and related metrics appear alongside the rest of your Health data.
